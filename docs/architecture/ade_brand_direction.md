# Brand direction

**Adelyr** is the current leading candidate for the platform name. It is connected to the founder's existing Adelier name and is intended to work across the suite, such as Adelyr Core, Adelyr Talent and Adelyr People.

The name is not yet finalized. The `.com` registry returned no registration record when checked, but registrar availability, purchase, price, trademark clearance, other product names and language checks remain unverified. Do not rename repositories, packages, domains or product interfaces until the brand decision is confirmed.
